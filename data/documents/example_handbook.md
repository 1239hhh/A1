# Example IT Support Handbook

## Account access
Employees who cannot sign in should first confirm that Caps Lock is off and that their account username is correct. If access still fails, contact the IT service desk and include the exact error message. Never send a password or one-time passcode in a support ticket.

## Laptop security
Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and automatic screen locking. Report a lost or stolen device to the security team as soon as possible. Do not install unapproved software or share company files through personal cloud accounts.

## Incident reporting
Report suspected phishing, unexpected MFA prompts, or possible data exposure to security@example.invalid. Include the time, affected service, and a short description. Do not click links or open attachments in a suspicious message.
